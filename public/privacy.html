Privacy Policy

Joe Daniels Connect • Gateway Church International
Effective Date: October 2026

1. Overview

Your privacy and spiritual security are paramount. This policy describes how Joe Daniels Connect collects, protects, and handles personal data across Web and Mobile applications for Gateway Church International under Apostle Joe Daniels.

2. Information Collected

  • Account Information: Full name, mobile phone number, email address, city/assembly, gender, and date of birth.

3. Data Protection & Security

All data is encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS encryption. Data is stored in secured, access-controlled cloud infrastructure. We never sell, rent, or monetize believer data.
